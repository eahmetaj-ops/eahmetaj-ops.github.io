01 / about

I like building tools that save people real time.

I'm a sophomore at Stevens studying Computer Science with a minor in Machine Learning, and I hold a 3.8 GPA. My coursework covers both sides of the field: the math behind machine learning (linear algebra, probability, multivariable calculus) and the systems underneath it (data structures, algorithms, computer architecture, ARM64 assembly).

At Pfizer I learned that a useful AI agent starts with listening. I met one-on-one with data scientists and business stakeholders, scoped where automation would help most, then built and tested agents in Databricks and Microsoft Copilot until they were accurate enough to rely on.

Outside of school I'm the technology associate at Pawsome Vita, a pet-care business in Jersey City, where I set up and support the systems the team uses every day. I've also spent years fundraising for maternal and child healthcare in Kosovo. I speak English and Albanian, plus some Spanish.
